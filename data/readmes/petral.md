# petral

Dashboard for physical crude oil trading desks. It pulls together benchmark prices (WTI, Brent, Dubai), calendar and crack spreads, inventories and forward curves in one place, with the source of every series shown next to it.

Forward curves are fitted with a Nelson-Siegel model in NumPy.

**Stack:** Next.js, TypeScript, Python, FastAPI, NumPy, Parquet

- Live: [petral.xyz](https://petral.xyz)
